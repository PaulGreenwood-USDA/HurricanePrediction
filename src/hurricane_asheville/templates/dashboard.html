The trained model loses to the naive "assume no change" baseline at every horizon ({% for h in ml.withheld_regression_horizons %}+{{ h }}h{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}), and loses by the widest margin on exactly the rows that matter: on days whose crest passes minor flood it is off by several feet where the baseline is off by about one.
